Build at a Pace That Works for You

You do not have to fund your entire construction project at once. HorizonView can help you plan manageable stages based on your budget and the work required.

Option 1: Pay All at Once

If you already have the full agreed construction budget, you can arrange to fund your project upfront.

  • Agree on the total project budget and scope of work.
  • Review the construction plan and payment terms.
  • Keep records of payments, expenses and receipts.
  • Track construction progress through your dashboard.

Option 2: Fund One Stage at a Time

Start with the amount you have available. Agree on the work that amount can reasonably cover, then plan the next stage.

  • Agree on the work and estimated cost.
  • Approve the stage budget before work begins.
  • Record deposits, expenses and receipts.
  • Review progress before funding the next stage.

Option 3: Save Toward a Target

If you are not ready to begin construction, you can plan toward an agreed starting amount first.

  • Set a target with your project team.
  • Keep a record of each amount received.
  • Review your progress toward the target.
  • Start only after the required funds and plans are agreed.

Construction funding is separate from HorizonView package fees. Before funds are collected or construction begins, the funding arrangements, approved work, payment records and applicable terms must be clearly agreed.
